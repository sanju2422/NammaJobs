🏛️ About NammaJobs – ನಮ್ಮಜಾಬ್ಸ್ ಬಗ್ಗೆ

Karnataka's Trusted Government Job Portal | ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ವಿಶ್ವಾಸಾರ್ಹ ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಉದ್ಯೋಗ ಪೋರ್ಟಲ್

NammaJobs (nammajobs.co.in) is a dedicated government job portal for Karnataka and India, providing daily updates on the latest government job notifications, admit cards, results, and recruitment news. Our mission is to make government job information accessible to every job seeker in Karnataka — in both English and Kannada languages.

We serve thousands of job seekers across Karnataka, from Bengaluru to Belagavi, Mysuru to Mangaluru — helping them find their dream government jobs quickly and easily. NammaJobs is a completely free service for all job seekers.
