Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us at Namma Jobs
📅 Last Updated: 1 May 2026

At Namma Jobs (nammajobs.co.in), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Namma Jobs and how we use it.

📋 Information We Collect

We do not require visitors to register or provide personal information to access our job listings. However, we may collect basic information automatically:

  • Browser Information: Browser type, language, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns
  • Device Information: Device type (mobile/desktop)
  • IP Address: For analytics and security purposes

🍪 Cookies

Like most websites, Namma Jobs uses cookies to improve user experience:

  • Theme preferences (Dark/Light mode)
  • Cached job data for faster loading
  • Anonymous analytics (Google Analytics)

You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

📊 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database for job listings
  • Google Analytics: Website traffic analysis
  • Google Search Console: Search performance tracking
  • Google AdSense (Future): Display advertisements

📢 Advertisements

In future, we may display third-party advertisements on our website. Third-party advertisers may use cookies and tracking technologies to deliver relevant ads. These advertisers may collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited on our website
  • Geographic location

We do not have access to or control over these cookies. Users can opt out of personalized ads through Google Ad Settings.

🔗 Job Information

All job notifications, admit cards, and results posted on Namma Jobs are sourced from publicly available official government and private company websites. We provide:

  • Direct links to official application portals
  • Verified information from official notifications
  • No collection of user applications or personal data

🛡️ Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our website does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us.

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
